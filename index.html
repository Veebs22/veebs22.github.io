MY PROJECTS


Queen of The Hill

Made From Scratch

Equal parts Rogue-Like, RTS, and Tower Defense, Queen of The Hill is an ant-themed Strategy/Adventure game I've been developing since 2024. Designed to showcase my full skill set as a developer, every detail, including the game engine, was created by me. The soundtrack, animations, story, art, sound effects, scripting, and behavioral algorithms... everything. The game is programmed in Java. No generative AI was used for any part of it. Aside from the P5 Processing library for rendering, every facet of this game was made by me and me alone. The goal was to experience the entire lifecycle of a game, from start to finish, from scratch.
View The Steam Page
Getting the Steam API to work without a game engine has been challenging, so at the moment, the game runs on Windows only. Thanks for your patience!

Throughout development I:

- Wrote over 20,000 lines of Java code.

- Created all the artwork in Photoshop, including 8 full-screen digital paintings and all sprite assets.

- Designed and iterated on game mechanics, organizing playtests to refine a fun and engaging gameplay loop.

- Animated particle effects, walk cycles, enemies, and UI elements.

- Programmed a realistic simulation of an ant colony, leveraging emergent behaviors.

- Developed performant algorithms for everything from collision detection to physics simulations and line-of-sight checks.

- Composed 10 original music tracks, ranging from moody ambience to epic boss themes.

Re-Re-Entry

Made With Unity

Made during a two-week game jam, Re-Re-Entry is themed after the classic arcade game; Galaga. I was one of four devs who worked on it. My team and I brought this game to life using Unity.
Play it in your browser on itch.io!

While the original enemy sprites weren't made by me, I did animate their explosions and burning states.

I also programmed and refined the Shotgun and Gattling gun weapons for the player.

I implemented UX enhancements like animated menus, scene transitions, camera shake, and enemy damage/tilt visuals.

The main menu and victory screens are my handiwork as well. The spinning trophies are animated by me, you also might catch that the final score tally is inspired by my love for the 'LEGO Star Wars' games.

My team and I effectively managed version control using Git throughout development.

In Too Deep

Current Work In Progress

In Too Deep is my newest game project, and this section will evolve as the build develops. As my most ambitious project to date, this section is designed to introduce the game, show the direction of the project, and provide space for documenting the game's development process.

In Too Deep is a horror game that sees the player trapped in a submarine, wherein they must listen for audio cues, stay on top of leaks, and repel sea monsters to survive.

In Too Deep is designed to capitalize on fear of the unknown. The gameplay loops and mechanics are structured to invite the player imagine what might be lurking just outside their field of view. The game mechanics have been through many many iterations. And will continue to be worked on as mentors weigh in and playtester feedback is gathered. current development is focusing on creating a stressful, claustrophobic, and immersive experience for players to navigate.
